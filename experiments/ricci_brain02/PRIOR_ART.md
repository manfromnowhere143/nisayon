[frontier review](../../work/experiments/ricci-brain-01-2026-11-24/FRONTIER_REVIEW.md),

reproduction. No competitor ranking or new mathematical contribution follows.

## Physical control and information

| Work and checked version | Formulation, implementation and information | Evaluation, resources and limitations | Relationship and reproduction decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feng et al., **Active Reachability Learning for Humanoid Recoverability Monitoring**, v1, 23 October 2026. [Methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33317v1) | Learns the stochastic finite-horizon recoverability of a specified fallback. Temporal-difference and Monte Carlo learning alternate with uncertainty-guided reach-avoid exploration; unknown regions are conservatively excluded. The G1 example uses a three-second horizon. | Analytical/stochastic benchmarks and a 29-DoF G1, with simulation and hardware evaluation. G1 training is reported at roughly six hours on an RTX 6000. Coverage, learned uncertainty and fixed fallback assumptions limit transfer; this is not a universally certified viability kernel. Methods, evaluation and limitations inspected. | Closest monitor alternative. Ricci would need task-preserving recovery, rather than standing alone, and measured coverage under model change. No training or code reproduction here; our numerical control defect must be resolved first. |
| Dang et al., **HULK**, v1, 6 October 2026. [Methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.08970v1) | Loaded centroidal MPC guides whole-body reinforcement learning. Two teachers are distilled; a capture-point barrier shapes one teacher during training. Privileged training information and scheduled-contact assumptions are explicit. | G1 simulation/hardware; comparisons include FALCON, Thor and AMO. Force, tracking and balance results differ by regime; not every metric favors the proposed controller. Training-time barrier shaping does not supply a deployed runtime safety certificate. Full compute reproduction not established here. | Strong predictive/learned baseline for later load handling. Its controller and task are not directly paired with Ricci's tray experiment. Method and result tables inspected; implementation compatibility and full training cost remain unchecked. |
| Byrd et al., **AdaptManip**, v2, 30 September 2026; v1, 16 February. [Methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14363v2) | RL locomotion with residual manipulation, recurrent object estimation and LiDAR navigation. Estimator uses available visual/proprioceptive history; training includes privileged state. | Simulation and hardware lifting/delivery. MuJoCo comparison reports 75% success against 61% with forward-kinematic estimation and 79% with oracle state. Two training stages take approximately a day each on one RTX 4090. Hardware demonstrations and finite test settings do not establish arbitrary contact robustness. | Particularly close task comparator. Our residual bank is not a recurrent object estimator. V2 methods, randomization and comparisons inspected; no training/download. Prior ledger's v1 metadata should not stand in for this revision. |
| Luo et al., **SONIC**, v4, 13 August 2026; first submission 11 November 2025. Science Robotics 11(117), eaed4592. [Methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.07820v4) | Motion-tracking PPO, multiple motion encoders and a common quantized latent interface; joint targets feed low-level control. Proprioceptive history and an asymmetric training critic are distinct information channels. | Large motion corpus and approximately 21,000 GPU hours. Held-out motion and hardware tests support the declared tracking system, not universal task recovery. Methods and evaluation inspected. Energy/formal-safety limitations matter. | A potential external whole-body execution backend, not evidence that Ricci needs equivalent training scale. The released [project](https://nvlabs.github.io/GEAR-SONIC/) offers a future integration route. No model or runtime reproduced here. |
| Sakçak, Timperi, Weinstein and LaValle, **A Mathematical Characterization of Minimally Sufficient Robot Brains**, v1, 17 August 2023. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.09041v1) | Internal/external transition systems, history-based information states, sufficient labelings and minimal information representations under stated equivalence assumptions. Filtering sufficiency and policy feasibility are separate questions. | Formal constructions and examples; not a calibrated hybrid-contact uncertainty model or morphology comparison. Key information-transition and sufficiency derivations inspected, not a claim of complete theorem reproduction. | Direct prior art against novelty claims for “only distinguish what changes a decision.” Ricci's open question would require physically realizable delayed observations, task feasibility and model coverage. HTML retrieval failed; PDF inspected. |
| Javdani et al., **Near Optimal Bayesian Active Learning for Decision Making**, AISTATS 2014, PMLR 33:430–438, 22–25 April. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v33/javdani14.pdf) | Overlapping decision regions; deterministic tests on finite hypotheses with a declared prior. Hyperedge Cutting uses adaptive-submodular structure for near-optimal expected test cost under its assumptions. | Approximate comparison learning and manipulator localization. Assumptions and main construction inspected. Guarantees do not automatically survive missing hypotheses, uncalibrated priors or a plant evolving during tests. | Established decision-focused acquisition must be a comparator when its premises hold. Ricci currently has neither a calibrated prior nor a validated complete hypothesis set. No invented prior or new selector was introduced. |
| Hartley et al., **Contact-Aided Invariant Extended Kalman Filtering for Robot State Estimation**, arXiv v2, 10 November 2019. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.09251), [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/0278364919894385) | Invariant filtering on a Lie group with contact information and explicit observability assumptions. | Current metadata checked; journal endpoint timed out. The existing mathematical ledger contains the earlier methodological review. No independent filter reproduction or covariance calibration in this slice. | Brain 01 implements a 21-state contact-aided error-state filter; it must not be relabeled as a faithful reproduction of this InEKF. |
| Tedrake, **Underactuated Robotics**, working notes retrieved 27 November 2026. [Contact](https://underactuated.mit.edu/contact.html), [robust control](https://underactuated.mit.edu/robust.html) | Hybrid contact models, guards/resets and model-dependent robust control formulations. | Expository derivations with explicit problem classes. Convex or smooth-model properties cannot be inherited by arbitrary hybrid contact simulation. Relevant sections inspected. | Motivates explicit guard semantics and separation of numerical QP feasibility from physical task feasibility. No claim that these notes certify the native controller. |

## What remains open

This review supports a restrained engineering choice: expose the existing
computation, identify its numerical and causal limits, and retain a complete
new execution whenever actions change. It does not establish a novel brain
architecture, a new information theorem or superiority over those systems.

The full RB-02 survey still needs primary-method treatment of decision-focused
dual control, directed information and morphology-dependent sufficiency, including
Sharbafi's compliance/delay work. The wider OpenAI, DeepMind, Physical Intelligence,
Figure, Tesla, Skild and Generalist review remains in the historical ledger and
is not claimed as newly completed here. Public demonstrations alone would not
resolve their proprietary information structures. No current result justifies
training a foundation model, changing the body, or adding an acquisition network.
