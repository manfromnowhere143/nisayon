# Challenge the acquisition saving with stronger conventional stopping

16 October 2026. This extension is specified after reading the first retained
N045 calculation and before executing this challenge. It is an exposed design
audit, not a new independent confirmation or an amendment hidden in the original
freeze. The initial protocol, program, results and command receipt remain intact.

The perfect-proxy cell first reaches the planned 80% support probability at
cap 57, with 22.2401 expected acquisitions and 85.7478% support. The ordinary
sequential test reaches it at cap 45, with 28.7671 expected acquisitions and
85.4346% support. Both original expectations charge trials even when no remaining
outcome could change the final support decision. That makes a stronger native
cost comparator necessary before interpreting the apparent saving.

1. Add decision-preserving futility to those exact cap-45 and cap-57 rules.
   Stop unresolved if current wealth times the largest permitted remaining
   factors cannot reach 20. Native maximum is 19/16. Prediction-audit maximum
   is 8/5, including an incorrect prediction; never assume perfect predictions
   to justify an early stop. Test both p=4/5 and p=19/20. Final support probability
   must equal the original exact value. Count all stopped and unresolved paths.
2. Enumerate all ordinary deterministic binomial rules with cap 1..80 and
   success threshold 1..cap. Retain only rules with exact null tail <=1/20 and
   planning-alternative tail >=4/5. Evaluate each with earliest deterministic
   stopping: support after reaching its success threshold, unresolved once too
   many failures make support impossible. Choose the minimum expected number
   of acquisitions under the declared p=19/20 planning model, breaking ties by
   smaller cap then smaller success threshold. This uses no observed robot
   outcome and makes no claim to optimize every sequential testing procedure.
3. Report all eligible rules and actual support probabilities. Compare costs
   only with their quality differences visible. For the minimum-cost native
   comparator and the perfect-proxy rule, compute the remaining integration
   allowance and break-even common context cost. If none remains, withdraw the
   corresponding cost opportunity. Retain preparation, predictor cost, context
   acquisition, reset, review and maintenance as unmeasured real costs.

Use exact rational arithmetic and the existing Python with site packages
disabled. No change to endpoints, runtime, original study cells, owner allocation
or engagements. The binomial tail and absorbing sequential calculation must
agree exactly for both the null and planning alternative on every eligible rule.
