**12 October 2026 · N027 · recorded before local analysis execution.**

The most consequential bottleneck remains a consequential owner decision with
complete costs. N025 supplies external adoption of one correction; N026 supplies
another delivered correction. Neither establishes a general advantage. Daniel
asks for deeper research and justified expansion. The next step uses a newly
located public rollout packet to examine a prerequisite for useful diagnosis:
whether the success definition actually identifies the choice being made.

## Questions and alternatives

1. **Which outcome matters?** Reaching a goal once, being there at a deadline,
   and sustaining it are different objectives. A predictor or larger sample
   cannot choose the owner's objective. A controller that stops at first success
   also defines a different execution from one that keeps acting.
2. **What supplies replication?** A reset seed is not a physical-state identity.
   Distinct stored states are not proof of stochastic independence. Keep the
   physical pairing and the sampling model separate.
3. **What does another component earn?** Compare with the released analysis
   first. Reproducing its answer does not justify replacing it with a new
   Nisayon backend or claiming lower cost.

## Chosen milestone and falsifier

Reproduce the release's entire fixed native-task comparison: two tasks, two
policy–controller pipelines, five arm settings and four batches of 256 scenes.
Use its unchanged `analyze_portable.py` as the conventional reference. In a
separate readback, verify the 80-cell inventory, source/plan/checkpoint metadata,
physical pairing and preserved technical repeat. Reconstruct both step-50 and
step-100 success bits from the saved states, and all 20 primary contrasts from
the saved sequences. Retain all four endpoint/setting envelopes and the original
sampling assumptions. Do not search additional horizons or thresholds.

The release reports a PushCube ranking reversal between ever-success by step 50
and success at step 50, with PickCube as a contrast. Reject local reproduction
if input integrity or physical pairing fails, required records are absent,
state-derived outcomes disagree, or any primary count/interval fails to match
the original within 1e-10. Exposed published results are already known: this is
reproduction of a recorded experiment, not fresh confirmation or blind discovery.

Also report a predeclared sensitivity using each 256-scene batch as one bounded
observation: four independent batches rather than 1,024 independent scenes.
For paired mean differences in [-1,1], use the same 20-comparison union bound and
Hoeffding radius sqrt(2 log(40/0.05)/n), with n=4. This changes the independence
assumption, not the data. It can be uninformative even when all batch signs agree;
it does not establish that the authors' scene-wise assumption is false.

## Resources, acceptance and next dependency

One public incident, at most four engineering hours and 1 GiB of additional local
artifacts; CPU readback only. Use a new Python 3.11.16 environment with the
release's NumPy 1.26.4 and SciPy 1.12.0, hash locked. Download only the selected
confirmation packet and necessary provenance. Run the native analyzer on a copy
because it writes beside its inputs. No model weights, policy execution,
training, simulator, GPU, hardware or Nisayon reserved conditions.

Acceptance requires complete reproduction or an explicit failed/invalid result,
unchanged source data, recorded commands and costs, and a concrete implication
for the next owner intake. If reproduced, record endpoint, stopping behavior,
sampling unit and physical pairing as prerequisites for that decision. No
general analyzer, failure monitor or adaptive selector is authorized by a
successful arithmetic reproduction. N015 still requires an owner choice and
matched complete-cost evidence before product expansion.
