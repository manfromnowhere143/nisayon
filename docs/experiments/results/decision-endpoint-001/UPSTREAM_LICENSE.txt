Scope of this licence, and what it does not cover
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The MIT terms above apply to the original code written for this work: the
evaluation harness and queues in `scripts/`, the decidability and replicate
tracks in `benchmark/`, the analysis scripts, and the Vulkan compatibility
layer.

They do NOT extend to:

  * Evaluation records and derived outputs. The per-episode `.jsonl` records,
    the `runs.jsonl` provenance logs, the `.npz` replay trajectories and the
    generated analysis tables are produced by running third-party policies in
    third-party simulators on third-party demonstration data. Any restriction
    inherited from those upstreams applies to them. See NOTICE.md.

  * Third-party components. Simulator source, policy checkpoints and datasets
    are not redistributed here and remain under their own licences, which take
    precedence over these terms for anything derived from them. See NOTICE.md.

  * The real-robot reference success rates compared against in Section 8.4 of
    the accompanying manuscript. These are published as part of the benchmark's
    own source, are cited rather than copied, and are not ours to license.
