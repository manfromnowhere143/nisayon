# N028: the next performance milestone

12 October 2026. **Continue deployment-change qualification. Earn an expansion
through one independently owned repair, then a separate repeat-use case.**
An online recovery subsystem remains a research option. Current evidence does
not justify building it or training another policy.

## Evidence and bottleneck

Nisayon has a real, bounded learned-policy simulation repair: B2 achieved
9/10 corrected tasks versus 0/10 unchanged. Ordinary source inspection exposes
the same remedy. Earlier comparisons tied the competent conventional arm.
Their fixed confirmation costs limit even free diagnosis to about 1.13× and
1.23× total speedups; another diagnostic feature cannot make those schedules
twice as efficient. See [B2](../b2-semantic-transfer-001/README.md) and the
[unused-information audit](../../UNUSED_AUDIT.md).

Arisole's maintainer adopted a correction, and we verified its published release.
That supports the usefulness of one software contribution. It does not establish
Nisayon product adoption, improved physical robot performance or lower complete
cost. [N027](../decision-endpoint-001/README.md) also shows why an endpoint must
precede a performance claim: first-hit and deadline success can reverse a choice.

**The most consequential missing evidence is a decision where our extra
experimentation changes the correction or avoids enough work to repay its cost.**
We have a local learned-policy runtime. More compute does not supply that case,
an independent owner, or an agreed meaning of success.

## Fresh research that changes the choice

| Primary source | Method and limitation | Consequence for Nisayon |
|---|---|---|
| [DREAM-Chunk v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.18589v1) | Chooses among frozen-policy action samples using learned latent predictions. Recovery depends on useful behavior in the candidate pool. Its real-robot table shows that increasing samples can increase latency and reduce success. | Compare against RTC, adaptive chunking and random switching. Test candidate coverage, prediction quality and execution latency separately before adding a selector. |
| [CoRe v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14822v1) | Imagines continuations, then restores robot/scene state. Imagined rollouts stop before contact. LIBERO restoration uses direct simulator state changes; other experiments include executable restoration. Episode reset and anchor rollback are strong comparators. | Count restoration feasibility, physical work and retained progress. A simulated state edit is not a deployable repair. Code availability was not established by the inspected paper/abstract. |
| [Cerebellar-inspired residual control v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07227v1) | Adds bounded adaptive corrections to a frozen policy using phase-indexed references and local errors in simulation. Bounded correction magnitude alone is not a general stability guarantee. | Not a drop-in improvement for our Lift checkpoint. It needs reference signals and a separate adaptation experiment; no compatible implementation was qualified here. |

These are author-reported results, not our replications or evidence that Nisayon
outperforms them. The recent [N026 comparison](../pose-contract-001/DECISION.md)
already covers major company directions; this review adds method and release
feasibility rather than another company survey.

## Alternatives and action

| Alternative | Decision |
|---|---|
| Train a larger policy or build a general recovery/world-model backend | Defer. No qualified target case or measured benefit covers the integration and training cost. |
| Repeat B2 or the consumed delay screen | Reject. They are exposed, spent cases; repetition cannot establish a new advantage. |
| Qualify one existing frontier comparator while pursuing an owner case | Execute the source prerequisite now. Reproduce its unchanged baseline only after a usable bundle and bounded execution protocol exist. |

The released [DREAM-Chunk Kinetix code](https://github.com/wenxichen2746/DREAM-Chunk-Kinetix/tree/0aae0757ca63895daf95dd60acfd3a562fd097a0)
is a relevant candidate, but its local Kinetix source is absent. A pinned archive
and native resolver check confirm that prerequisite blocker. We requested the
intended revision and one existing flow/RSSM evaluation bundle in
[issue #1](https://github.com/wenxichen2746/DREAM-Chunk-Kinetix/issues/1).
This opens a reproducible research route without starting training. It does not
make the comparator executable yet or refute the paper.

## Milestone, falsifier and resources

The next value milestone is **one owner-confirmed correction on a consequential
deployment change**, compared with an equally informed conventional workflow.

Acceptance requires:

1. Bind the working/changed source, checkpoint and resolved settings to the
   existing failure. Freeze the owner's outcome, deadline, stopping behavior,
   permitted intervention and target population before new confirmation.
2. Run the ordinary source/configuration check first. Give both workflows the
   same evidence, useful upstream remedies and measured resource ceilings.
3. Freeze one correction and test fresh conditions. Choose the sample unit,
   practical improvement/non-inferiority margins and uncertainty procedure from
   that task before seeing results. Record task progress and constraint failures;
   returning unresolved is admissible. A software test pass is insufficient.
4. Report setup, diagnosis, unsuccessful trials, confirmation, owner review,
   integration and maintenance costs. Count all assigned cases. An improvement
   in task success alone does not establish a diagnosis-cost advantage.
5. Require a separate untouched decision before expanding the engine. The older
   twofold-cost target remains an ambition, not an achieved or newly allocated run.

**Falsifier:** a capable conventional workflow reaches the same accepted repair
with equal or lower complete cost, or the correction loses its benefit under
fresh task conditions. Do not expand the disputed subsystem in either case.

For a later recovery branch, first ask whether feasible candidates can recover
the task at all, whether a selector beats random/ordinary selection, and whether
the advantage survives measured latency. An oracle using future outcomes could
only bound candidate opportunity; it would not be a deployable comparator.

Current resources suffice for CPU/source work. N015 retains one incident and
four hours of initial inspection, with no fresh policy/training/GPU/hardware
allocation. A usable research bundle would need its own frozen environment and
costed protocol. No new cloud spending or hardware purchase is recommended now.

Daniel's highest-value contribution is one introduction to a robotics engineer
who owns a failure, can share permitted existing records, and can verify a
correction in their environment. They need not transfer private weights or give
us hardware control. A public checkpoint bundle supports method research;
it cannot substitute for evidence of an expensive recurring owner decision.
