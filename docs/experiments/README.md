# Experiments

Nisayon began with a deployment regression that could be reproduced and
corrected in a simulator. The next question was whether its checks improved
the work of finding and confirming a repair. Two comparisons have now tested
that question at a bounded development scope. Both tied on accepted repairs;
the arm with additional checks took more measured trial time.

Work through N045 on 16 October adds native software corrections, finite probes
for causes or declared decisions, current research, and a bounded qualification
of prediction audits against complete confirmation cost.
The 23 October Kornia comparison adds separately selected software reuse with
matched decision quality. The 24 October contract experiment reuses frozen
executable semantics on another proposal and retains the exposure boundary. The
owner comparison and complete-cost advantage remain open. A new
[active-effort recorder](results/owner-effort-2026-10-24/README.md) qualifies
measurement of owner setup, review and full declared costs; no owner trial has
started. The tables below
connect the trajectory to its retained evidence.

## Results and what changed

The [28 October residual-monitoring audit](results/residual-monitoring-2026-10-28/README.md)
reanalyses 927 released robot episodes. It separates task success, missed
failures, valid-success retention and missing evidence across all available
SO-101 policy/task/session cohorts. Cross-policy associations remain exploratory;
the reusable summary does not grant deployment acceptance.

The [incomplete-label extension](results/incomplete-monitoring-2026-10-28/README.md)
quantifies what the same records leave unresolved. With known labels assumed
correct, the 65 ambiguous community outcomes permit a conditional miss rate
of 4.5%–32.7%, compared with 6.7% among labelled failures. These finite bounds
preserve missing evidence and do not establish population risk.

The [28 October native capture comparison](results/native-capture-cost-2026-10-28/README.md)
removes an extra full-model copy. Six paired runs preserve all recorded states
and image bytes while reducing median process time by 18–19%. A faster persistent
buffer fails the memory limit and remains rejected. This compares two Nisayon
implementations; complete owner-cost advantage remains unresolved.

The [24 October native continuation](results/native-continuation-2026-10-24/README.md)
qualifies two optimizer updates and fresh-process checkpoint continuation with
published LongWAM IDM weights. All 25 gates pass across two synthetic seeds;
the next update matches on both ranks. The combined candidate, instrumented
recipe, failed prefixes and complete checkpoint-retention record remain
distinct from training quality, owner adoption and comparative cost.

The [24 October read-witness experiment](results/owner-read-witness-2026-10-24/README.md)
exposes an omitted normalization-data dependency with an unmodified OpenPI
loader. New native plans retain owner-file read attempts, block observed
undeclared inputs and invalidate reuse when declared data changes. Initial and
fresh vectors confirm the controls; this is a constructed software boundary,
not an owner-cost comparison or complete dependency discovery.

The [24 October OpenPI composition](results/openpi-image-composition-2026-10-24/README.md)
qualifies two existing authors' image fixes together. All 48 common checks and
both native helper tests pass on initial and fresh inputs. A shape-correct,
pixel-incorrect control is rejected. This is software evidence, with owner
acceptance and complete-cost value still unmeasured.

The scripts shared known remedies. The model comparison used one known incident,
with equal information and budgets. Neither establishes unseen-fault performance
or a complete-cost advantage. Human effort and provider charges remain unknown.
Reports below retain their original chronology, including dependencies since
resolved. The [release notes](../RELEASE.md) give the integrated state and exact
historical source versions. Fresh random conditions do not make a known fault unseen.
