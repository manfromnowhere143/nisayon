# Prospective owner and reuse comparison

Owner comparison status: prepared, not enrolled or executed. This preserves N015/N086's product
test and the [current native comparison](experiments/results/native-sdk-2026-10-23/README.md).
The XPolicyLab development replay is exposed and cannot serve as the untouched
reuse case. The SDK's `adopt` result covers only its declared software relation.

## Intake and selection

Use one current owner-approved change outside the separate LeRobot lane. Retain
the owner's decision question, working reference, changed implementation,
existing failure records, allowed execution environment and applicable task
criterion. Resolve ambiguous semantics with the owner before treating a
difference as a defect. Cap initial intake at four hours and retain an unresolved
outcome if the case cannot become testable within that ceiling.

Before execution, freeze both arms' native tools, information, model access,
allowed interventions and complete budgets. The competent baseline is the
owner's native tools plus a capable agent. An intentionally weak script is not
that baseline. Record all material already exposed to the builder and identify
who owns any fresh or reserved answers. A source worktree or file permission is
not an evaluation-isolation boundary.

## Decide before observing

Freeze the outcome-quality criterion, intervention-selection rule, failure
controls, subgroup requirements, task acceptance rule and stopping conditions.
Preserve the existing 32-pair fresh confirmation obligation wherever it applies;
the portable JSON-call adapter does not discharge it. Action-changing experiments
must recompute downstream state and observations using a qualified native task
adapter. A candidate that only stops the robot does not repair its task.

Report software qualification and deployment acceptance separately. Account for
invalid, failed, unresolved, inconclusive, supported and contradicted results.
Unexecuted confirmation remains unexecuted. Never reinterpret an original
numerical failure as a pass after relaxing its tolerance; a changed precision
policy is a new declared candidate.

## Measure the entire comparison

Retain intake, setup, adapter edits, diagnostic trials, retries, model usage,
confirmation, review, adoption and maintenance. Report human effort, provider
charges, compute and wall time in their original units. Reconcile nested clocks
without adding them twice. Missing costs stay unknown. The historical target
is at least a twofold reduction in complete cost at matched decision quality;
meeting a partial runtime target is insufficient.

The new adapter's process startup and environment inspection are measured work.
A more elaborate record can be slower than native tooling. Equal or worse
complete cost at matched quality is a valid negative product result, not a
reason to hide the comparator or reclassify setup as free.

## Test reuse separately

After the first case, freeze the reusable procedure and applicability map.
An evaluator who has not exposed the second case's answers selects a separate
incident within the declared scope. Preserve every adaptation, failed attempt
and request for help as part of the reuse cost. Compare against equally informed
native tools and agent access. A changed population, scorer, state contract or
dependency requires new evidence; a retrieved verdict cannot grant acceptance.

If the second case requires another bespoke subsystem, revise the product
scope. Only after this gate should selection research compare expert rules,
the finite planner, procedure retrieval and a learned selector. Use equal
information and budgets, including no-memory, stale-memory and restart controls.
Hosted execution needs a demonstrated remote execution or shared review need.

The next missing input is a real owner case. The 24 October check of the existing
deployment inquiry and two public invitations supplied no new decision records.
No owner has been enrolled in this comparison. The public SDK integration
checks are a separate software task and do not substitute for this experiment.

The [23 October GR00T qualification](experiments/results/groot-statistics-2026-10-23/README.md)
uses a current public author proposal and native CPU checks. It establishes a
bounded software result and a retained reuse procedure, without owner enrollment
or an independent complete-cost comparison. Its fresh generated inputs are not
the untouched second incident.

The subsequent [Kornia comparison](experiments/results/untouched-reuse-2026-10-23/README.md)
tests the unchanged SDK and frozen procedure on a separately selected public
software incident. Both isolated solver arms meet the same frozen rubric: 5/5
correct decisions, no false acceptance among four unsuitable variants, and a
correction passing 32 reserved conditions. Both workflows reproduce. Nisayon
takes 2.7% more solver time with 24.4% fewer counted tokens. Setup, retries and
review are retained; provider charges, active human effort and owner costs remain
unknown. This supports bounded software reuse without discharging the owner
comparison or twofold complete-cost target.

The [24 October contract reuse](experiments/results/contract-reuse-2026-10-24/README.md)
then bound an unchanged executable contract to another current proposal, with
matched scoped decisions and unchanged replay. The native workflow remained
faster. This establishes reusable qualification within the retained selection
boundary; it does not measure avoided owner setup or review.

## Observe owner work prospectively

The [active-effort example](../examples/qualification/owner_effort/README.md)
records explicit human intervals, rejects conflicting allocations and retains
open or interrupted work. It composes phased effort, declared rates and charges
with the existing cost-target evaluator. Its initial ledger contains unknowns
and pending owner reviews. The [2026 primary-source review](experiments/results/owner-effort-2026-10-24/SOURCES.md)
explains why elapsed agent time and test success cannot supply these observations.

Freeze all eligible cases, selection or decline reasons, assigned workflows,
reviewer identities, quality controls and stopping rules before observing the
comparison. Give both arms equal semantic knowledge, capable agents, budgets and
opportunities for feedback. Charge the preparation of each usable deliverable,
including a native review packet comparable to the portable Nisayon packet.
Do not compare native execution alone against execution plus export and review.

Measure active owner setup and review separately from active engineering work,
wall clocks, provider usage and compute. Include capture overhead, preparation,
adaptation, retries, delivery, integration and the agreed maintenance horizon.
Unknown historical preparation remains unknown. Shared preparation is charged
once per arm for comparison and once in the actual study total. Candidate-only
development stays in the candidate arm; no assumed future amortization.

An owner who sees one solution cannot become untouched for the same decision's
second workflow. Randomizing order alone does not remove that carryover. For a
single-owner pilot, show one prospectively assigned packet first and record
subsequent exposed review separately; leave its unobserved counterfactual effort
unknown. A second qualified reviewer can support a paired descriptive comparison,
with reviewer differences disclosed. A causal owner-effort estimate needs enough
prospectively assigned comparable decisions, accounting for owner/task blocks
and incomplete assignments. Do not infer a population effect from two cases or
use a bootstrap over dependent controls as independent replication.

Require the owner to judge whether the delivered decision resolves the frozen
question, with unresolved choices permitted when appropriate. Record review
questions and rework; neither a passing test nor agreement between model
reviewers fills this acceptance field. Record failures against the full assigned
denominator. Freeze the reusable package after the initial decision, and retain
the separate selector's exposure and applicability record before subsequent use.
The historical twofold complete-cost target and fresh-confirmation obligations
remain unchanged. The measurement tool is ready; owner enrollment, active effort
observations and an accepted deployment decision remain outstanding.
