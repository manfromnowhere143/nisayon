# Qualification under change: the next evidence

9 November 2026. Nisayon's research question is whether an engineer can preserve
useful evidence after a consequential change, obtain the missing tests, and
reach an equally defensible decision with less complete work. The current engine
implements parts of that workflow. Competitive superiority and owner savings
remain unestablished.

## What the source review changes

The [source register](experiments/results/main-refresh-2026-11-09/research-sources.json)
records exact paper revisions, inspected methods, selected code identities and
limitations. This is a selective review, not replication or an exhaustive ranking.

EC² and the existing finite decision planner motivate preserving uncertainty
between hypotheses that imply the same decision. Supplied hypotheses and labels
remain premises; no surviving hypothesis means model mismatch. Dependency-aware
reuse and hash-bound records are established engineering, not a new theory.
The register also preserves the reviewed OpenAI evaluation and Google world-model
limitations. Agreement between assessors or models is not independent empirical
verification, and simulated success does not establish physical transfer.

## What has been earned locally

New native plans with observed unbound external reads remain unresolved. The
[calibration example](../examples/qualification/executable_checks/README.md)
declares a prospective input digest, provenance, permitted use and export
permission. Trusted handlers consume the captured snapshot. An incomplete
dependency model still cannot license reuse.

Optional partial execution retains only checks independent of unknown inputs.
Review reconstructs completed receipts inside unfinished runs. A pinned portable
packet can enter a new evidence root; a new execution must bind current inputs,
reassess applicability and execute required confirmation. These are software
capabilities with explicit limits, documented in the
[applicability contract](EVIDENCE_APPLICABILITY.md).

The local development evidence does not establish an efficiency gain. Three
staged-input controls matched competent native reuse and full rerun; staging
used four, six and five check executions, compared with four after waiting for
all inputs. An earlier eight-revision calibration comparison stopped on
measurement defects and remains stopped. The Kornia comparison used 2.7% more
solver time and 24.4% fewer counted tokens, with complete costs unknown.

## The next consequential comparison

Use the existing [owner protocol](OWNER_COMPARISON.md), including its four-hour
initial intake ceiling. A current participating owner, permitted records,
working and changed references, task semantics, qualified reference and feasible
budget are required. Interest or an adopted upstream patch is not enrollment.

Freeze the population, source identities, allowed information, interventions,
quality rule, costs and stopping conditions before observing outcomes. Compare
native tools plus a capable agent, ordinary structured experiment records, and
the existing Nisayon workflow. Give each the same semantic information and a
review deliverable sufficient for the same decision. A new selector needs a
recurring decision problem and evidence that simpler methods leave useful work
to remove.

Count all assigned cases, false clearance, useful valid decisions, unnecessary
invalidation and unresolved results. Charge setup, adapters, acquisition,
failures, retries, confirmation, review, integration and maintenance. Keep human
effort, wall time, compute and money separate. Unknown cost is not zero.

Equal-quality conventional work at equal or lower complete cost rejects the
efficiency claim for that case. A dependency shift that licenses the wrong
decision rejects the claimed applicability scope. After a qualifying first case,
freeze the procedure and use a separately selected untouched incident with an
exposure record. No enrolled owner or such product result is established by this
source update.
