# Research that changes the architecture

The general-purpose humanoid remains the objective. This is a targeted primary-source
review as of 24 November 2026, not an exhaustive survey or a claim to have surpassed
other laboratories. The six requested sources and their transfer limits are in
[PRIOR_ART.md](PRIOR_ART.md). Additional retained identities and access failures are
in [frontier-sources.json](frontier-sources.json).

The most consequential finding is direct prior art on learning a humanoid fallback
policy's recoverability envelope. A recovery map, uncertainty-aware fallback,
contact-aware recovery, or hierarchical control cannot by itself support a Ricci
novelty claim. A more specific question remains worth testing: under a shared
observation and control budget, when does distinguishing support failure from load
motion change the outcome, and how late can the useful information arrive?

| Primary source; methods/evaluation read | What the evidence supports | Consequence for Ricci |
|---|---|---|
| [Feng et al., Active Reachability Learning, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33317v1), §§II–V | Learns a **given fallback policy's** stochastic finite-horizon liveness using TD+Monte Carlo anchors and uncertainty-directed exploration. G1 monitoring uses a 69D state, including estimated height/velocity on hardware. Humanoid tests use a three-second horizon, held-out seeds, OOD push states and closed-loop disturbance sweeps; hardware transfer is qualitative. The authors explicitly state that learned solutions remain uncalibrated and coverage depends on exploration authority. | Closest prior art. Measure task-preserving outcomes and effective intervention delay; do not relabel standing recovery as tray completion. An empirical monitor is not a universal viability certificate. Active exploration is outside this milestone. |
| [RecoverFormer, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.22911v1), §§III–VI | A causal history encoder, latent recovery modes and contact-affordance outputs feed joint targets in G1 MuJoCo. Inputs include base velocity and distances to candidate environmental contact regions. Experiments report push recovery, walls and dynamics mismatch, with success defined by return to standing. The displayed tables do not establish a matched strong-baseline or component-ablation advantage. Text and Table III give differing mismatch-trial counts; training/evaluation push durations also differ. | Relevant precedent, not a result to inherit. Resolve protocol details before replication. Our proprioception/load sensors lack its contact-region distances; task completion and diagnosis timing require separate tests. |
| [SONIC, v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.07820v4), §§2.1, 3.2, 3.5–3.7, Supplement S1.2 | PPO motion tracking maps several command formats into a shared quantized interface and outputs joint positions. It uses proprioceptive/action history and randomized dynamics. Evaluation distinguishes unseen motion categories from new repetitions and includes external motion datasets. Tracking success uses height deviations rather than absolute global path error; some ablation tables report a single evaluation per configuration. | Borrow the stable motion interface and distinct generalization splits. Natural motion is not evidence of load recovery or global trajectory correctness. Learned tracking would need its own contact, timing and task-progress qualification. |
| [Joint force/footstep iLQR-MPC + WBC, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.04204v1), §§III–VI | Explicit reduced dynamics optimize contact forces and footstep locations; a constrained WBC tracks them. G1 MuJoCo tests include slow walking and directional push recovery. The reported CPU MPC averages about 8.9 ms and sometimes exceeds its 10 ms replanning period. **Disturbance wrench is assumed known.** | A useful inspectable successor to fixed foot trajectories. It is not a fair sensor-only baseline without replacing that privileged wrench and counting overruns. Contact-switching and friction uncertainty still need qualification. |
| [Lee et al., ISSf-CBF whole-body control, v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.25546v1), §§3–6 | A kinematic velocity safety filter feeds dynamics control on 33-DoF TOCABI; pelvis IMU and ankle F/T sensing support estimation. Comparisons include CBF variants under a 20% simulated mass mismatch and hardware boundary/collision tasks. Transfer relies on bounding/tuning the tracking discrepancy; conflicting constraints can make the QP infeasible. Terrain/friction balance failure remains a stated limitation. | Preserve an independent command supervisor, but qualify its actual terminal behavior. Torque clipping or a feasible instantaneous QP does not prove recoverability. Do not attach a safety theorem whose assumptions our model has not established. |
| [Physical Intelligence, π0.7](https://www.pi.website/download/pi07.pdf), §§VII–IX and evaluation appendices | Rich prompt metadata, image subgoals, observation history and flow-based action chunks combine robot and non-robot experience. Experiments separate dexterity, instruction following, embodiment transfer and composition. Fixed/mobile manipulators use cameras, PD/IK and 20/50 Hz control. Autonomous historical evaluation data is used in training, while generalization-focused evaluation tasks are excluded. | Keep capability/split lineage explicit. A later skill proposer can use typed intent and context, but must not issue unchecked torques or write its own success labels. No VLA is installed here. |
| [MEM, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03596v1), §§III–IV, Appendix B | Short visual history and learned compressed language memory support long tasks on camera-equipped manipulators. Evaluation uses ten rollouts per policy/task or recipe, component ablations and separately described adaptation conditions. Generated memory labels use subtask annotations and success/failure indicators during training. | Separate fast physical belief from long-lived task memory. A remembered action is not a measured outcome. Preserve provenance and uncertainty when a future learned memory updates task state. |
| [Real-Time Chunking, v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.07339v2), §§3–4, 6 | Asynchronous inpainting preserves the committed action prefix while a diffusion/flow policy predicts its continuation. Evaluation includes 12 stochastic Kinetix tasks with simulated delays and six physical bimanual tasks with injected inference delay. Methods compare throughput and final task score; physical legged locomotion is outside those tests. | Later learned skills must respect already executed actions, packet age and cancellation boundaries. Count time to effective actuation; smooth-looking chunk transitions are insufficient. |
| [Figure, Helix 2.5](https://www.figure.ai/news/helix-2-5-zero-shot-30-home-generalization), evaluation and appendix | Company-authored evaluation of three behavior policies across 30 unseen homes, with held-out objects, fixed checkpoints and declared full-task criteria. Holding downstream data and training fixed, the report attributes a 9% to 56% success increase to Index pretraining. Tasks are specified through training elsewhere. | A useful standard for configuration isolation and task completeness. It is a company report with its stated tasks and data assumptions, not an independently replicated general-purpose ranking. |
| [Gemini Robotics 2 safety report](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini-robotics/Gemini-Robotics-2-Safety.pdf), §§2.1–2.6 | Evaluates instruction following, spatial judgments, safety-tool calling and feasibility awareness. Some agentic tests use recorded video and an LLM-emulated VLA confidence tool. Structured-message stop-tool accuracy is a classification result; it does not measure physical stopping distance or contact stability. | An independent physical supervisor and measured outcomes must remain outside semantic confidence. A correctly emitted stop request does not establish a safe stop. |
| [Meta, V-JEPA 2, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.09985v1), §§3–4, Appendix B | An action-conditioned latent predictor supports image-goal planning on Panda arms in two labs. Comparisons use low-resolution monocular cameras, operational-space control and ten trials per condition. Planning is blocking; the reported V-JEPA setup takes about 16 seconds per action. Camera placement, long horizons and image-goal specification are limitations. | World-model predictions can propose futures but cannot become evaluator truth. A model useful for slow manipulation is not automatically usable in a subsecond balance loop. Validate causal rollout errors and compute cost before adding it. |

[Tesla's public AI/Optimus overview](https://www.tesla.com/AI) was checked for public
technical grounding. It did not supply a reproducible contact/load uncertainty
benchmark or sufficient methods for a matched comparison. Direct archival access
returned HTTP 403; that failure is retained. No private Optimus architecture or
performance is inferred from public demonstrations. The same standard applies to
every company and to Ricci.

The architecture decision is to retain three independently testable functions:
physical state estimation, feasible action selection, and evidence evaluation.
Broader perception, learned motion, semantic memory and world models can eventually
connect through typed interfaces. Their admission criterion is a measured gain on
fresh tasks under timing and constraint budgets. Model size and a plausible video
are not that criterion.
