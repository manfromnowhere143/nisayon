Preserve producer bytes when reusing recorded check evidence

A valid JSON observation recorded through record_check could execute as reused
but fail subsequent review: execute_checks reserialized the observation, changing
the byte identity required by its receipt. Preserve the already verified bytes
for reused results. Newly executed results keep their existing serialization.

An independent regression covers compact, CRLF, reordered/indented and unterminated
JSON through review, portable export/import and continued execution. All eight
reuse cases failed before and pass after. Calibration invalidation, changed-artifact
reacquisition and unknown-input refusal remain effective. All 11 controls pass;
the qualification directory has 1,089 passes and eight native-module skips.
Ruff and diff checks pass. The three-cell dependency-aware native comparison
reaches identical decisions/actions after the correction, with no observed cost
advantage. No UI, schema, dependency or CI change. No hosted or device validation.
