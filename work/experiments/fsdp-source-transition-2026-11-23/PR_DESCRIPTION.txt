## Use case

My LongWAM FSDP2 loading and recovery work uses CPU offload. The temporary full state dictionary can remain referenced while assignment prepares local shards. This follows the ownership correction in #4447.

## Change

Carry the optional consume_full_state_dict behavior onto upstream bd0703e3390ebb2b4eb1db5a0f1283f8edbc6091. Only the owning preparation call enables it. Borrowed dictionaries remain unchanged. Preserve the newer managed-parameter, ignored-parameter and buffer loading behavior.

## Validation

A frozen two-process CPU/Gloo probe executes the exact reviewed loader function, native broadcasts, DTensor distribution and actual assignment. Twenty development conditions per rank cover source layouts, empty/uneven shards, mixed state, float32/bfloat16 and borrowed ownership.

Unchanged upstream produces 16 rank-level ownership failures while preserving values; the candidate passes. Eight fresh confirmation conditions per rank also pass. Clearing after assignment produces the same 16 failures. The old published loader errors on the new mixed-state control.

The source, probe, scorer, raw observations and offline readback are retained in the accompanying qualification packet. run_probe.py accepts --phase development or --phase confirmation; verify.py reads existing evidence without native execution.

CUDA offload/pinned memory, full prepare_model integration, training and memory-performance measurements were not run. Historical GPU/RSS results do not qualify this revision.
