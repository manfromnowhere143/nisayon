STATIC SHOULDER MODEL
This UI extends the retained one-arm arithmetic to two independent horizontal
arms with a CHOSEN vertical tray-load split. The split is not solved from grasp
geometry or measured reactions. For each arm assume 4 kg at 0.25 m horizontal
moment arm. Gravity = 9.81 m/s^2. Tray plus payload initial mass = 2 kg; horizontal
shoulder-to-support moment arm = 0.55 m; left load share alpha = 0.5.
tau_left = 4*9.81*0.25 + alpha*payload_kg*9.81*reach_m
tau_right = 4*9.81*0.25 + (1-alpha)*payload_kg*9.81*reach_m
Initial result = 15.2055 N m per shoulder. Arm contribution = 9.81 N m each.
The retained one-arm case assigns the entire 2 kg payload to one arm and yields
20.601 N m. Neither number is a rated torque, physical outcome or motor choice.
Diagram is schematic, not to scale. Independent moment arms do not define
anatomical geometry. Dynamics, friction, actuator/transmission losses, contacts,
thermal behaviour, fatigue and safety factors are outside this model.
